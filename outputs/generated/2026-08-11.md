Hackers have obtained about 607,000 records in a cyber-attack on the Department for Education (DfE) in England.

The data taken includes telephone numbers and email addresses relating to individuals and organisations but does not include bank details or other sensitive information.

The DfE confirmed the incident and said it is working closely with the National Cyber Security Centre and the National Crime Agency (NCA).

The data protection risk to individuals is not considered high and it is understood the attack was contained quickly.

The Turing Scheme portal - a programme providing funding for international education - and the DfE online help desk have both been affected, but are expected to be operating normally later this week.

The DfE has referred itself to the Information Commissioner's Office.

"We have robust processes in place to protect information and took swift action to contain this incident", a DfE spokesperson said.

"The information involved is limited to customer service contact details relating to individuals and organisations. No other data has been accessed".

It stressed the 607,000 figure relates to the total number of records affected, not individuals.

An NCA spokesperson said: "We are aware of an incident affecting the Department for Education and are working with partners to understand the circumstances and impact."

## 长难句理解

> The data protection risk to individuals is not considered high and it is understood the attack was contained quickly.

**语法要点：**无人称被动结构 (it is understood that...)

**分析：**该句为并列句，由 and 连接两个分句。前一分句使用被动语态 is not considered high，说明评估结果；后一分句使用 it is understood that... 的无人称被动结构，其中 that 被省略，表示据信、据悉。这种结构在新闻报道中很常见，用于客观陈述。

**翻译：**对个人而言数据保护风险被认为不高，据悉攻击已迅速得到控制。


> The Turing Scheme portal - a programme providing funding for international education - and the DfE online help desk have both been affected, but are expected to be operating normally later this week.

**语法要点：**复杂主语、插入语及不定式进行体

**分析：**本句结构复杂。主语由两个并列成分组成：the Turing Scheme portal 和 the DfE online help desk，中间用插入语解释图灵计划；谓语部分 have both been affected 与 but are expected... 并列。插入语中 providing funding for international education 为现在分词短语作后置定语。后半句包含被动式 are expected to be operating，使用了不定式的进行体表示将来预期。

**翻译：**图灵计划门户——一个为国际教育提供资金的计划——以及教育部在线帮助台都受到了影响，但预计本周晚些时候将恢复正常运行。


> It stressed the 607,000 figure relates to the total number of records affected, not individuals.

**语法要点：**省略 that 的宾语从句及否定补充结构

**分析：**该句中 it stressed 后面跟了一个没有引导词 that 的宾语从句（the 607,000 figure relates to ...）。在后半部分，not individuals 用逗号分隔，补充说明受影响的是记录总数而非个人总数。这种省略 that 的用法在正式报道中常见。

**翻译：**该部门强调，60.7万这一数字指的是受影响的记录总数，而非个人数量。


> Around a quarter of further education institutions (24%) reported experiencing a breach or attack at least weekly according to the government's most recent Cyber Security Breaches Survey.

**语法要点：**report + 动名词结构及 according to 信息来源表达

**分析：**本句中 reported experiencing 是动词 report 后接动名词作宾语的用法，表示报告某事件。at least weekly 作频率状语，according to... 为介词短语引出信息来源。句子结构为主语 + 谓语 (reported) + 动名词短语 + 状语。

**翻译：**政府最新的《网络安全漏洞调查报告》显示，约有四分之一（24%）的继续教育机构报告每周至少经历一次漏洞或攻击。

## 习语与地道表达

**weak spots** 薄弱环节，漏洞

通常指系统、计划或组织中容易被攻击或出问题的部分，常见于网络安全、商业等语境。

例句：The audit revealed several weak spots in our financial controls.


**watchdog** 监察机构，监督人

比喻义，指负责监督某一行业或机构活动的组织或个人，如 watchdog organization。

例句：A consumer watchdog has warned about misleading advertisements.


**for fun** 为了好玩，闹着玩地

常用在口语中，表示做某事的动机是娱乐而非认真目的；在原文中指学生黑客攻击学校是出于好玩。

例句：He learned to code just for fun, not for a job.
