The NHS says to check your breasts, external or chest about once a month.

That helps you learn what looks and feels normal for you, making it easier to notice any abnormal changes.

Breasts can naturally look and feel a bit different at different phases of your menstrual cycle, and change a bit as you age.

a lump or swelling in your breast, chest or armpit

a change in the skin of your breast, such as dimpling (it may look like orange peel) or redness, which may be harder to see on black or brown skin

a change in size or shape of one or both breasts, or either side of your chest – it's common for breasts to be different sizes, but check for any changes that are not normal for you

nipple discharge (if you're not pregnant or breastfeeding), which may have blood in it

a change in the shape or look of your nipple, such as it turning inwards (inverted nipple) or a rash appearing on it (it may look like eczema)

Look at your breasts or chest in a mirror to look for any changes. Start with your arms by your sides and then raise them up.

Feel around each breast or side of your chest in a circular motion all the way up to your collarbone and under each armpit. Use a mix of light and firmer pressure. Do not press so hard it hurts.

## 长难句理解

> Signs of breast cancer are not always a lump in the traditional sense, so what should you look for?

**语法要点：**否定句 + 介词短语作状语；so 引导结果分句；wh-疑问句

**分析：**主句是否定判断句，`in the traditional sense` 作状语限定“肿块”的理解；`so` 引出结果性提问，`what should you look for` 是特殊疑问句，用 `should` 表达建议。

**翻译：**乳腺癌的迹象并不总是传统意义上的肿块，那么你应该留意什么？


> Actress Lucy Davis, who played Dawn Tinsley in the original UK version of the popular TV series The Office, has revealed she has incurable breast cancer.

**语法要点：**非限制性定语从句；省略 that 的宾语从句

**分析：**主句为 `Actress Lucy Davis has revealed`，`who` 引导非限制性定语从句补充身份信息，`revealed` 后接省略 `that` 的宾语从句。

**翻译：**女演员露西·戴维斯曾出演英国原版热门电视剧《办公室》中的唐·廷斯利，她透露自己患有无法治愈的乳腺癌。


> That helps you learn what looks and feels normal for you, making it easier to notice any abnormal changes.

**语法要点：**wh-宾语从句；现在分词短语作结果状语

**分析：**主句为 `That helps you learn`，`what looks and feels normal for you` 作 `learn` 的宾语从句，`making` 现在分词短语作结果状语，表示前句动作产生的后果。

**翻译：**这有助于你了解自己正常的外观和触感，从而更容易发现任何异常变化。


> Feel around each breast or side of your chest in a circular motion all the way up to your collarbone and under each armpit.

**语法要点：**祈使句；方式状语；并列介词短语表空间范围

**分析：**祈使句以 `feel` 开头，`around each breast or side of your chest` 表示检查范围，`in a circular motion` 说明方式，`all the way up to... and under...` 是介词短语补充具体位置。

**翻译：**以画圈的方式触摸每侧乳房或胸部侧面，一直向上到锁骨，并包括每侧腋下。

## 习语与地道表达

**in the traditional sense** 传统意义上；按照通常的理解

常用于指出某概念并不符合人们通常的理解，语气中性，常见于说明文和医学语境。

例句：The test result was not a failure in the traditional sense, but it did not meet the expected standard.


**as such** 严格来说；本身

用于对先前说法进行修正或限定，常放在否定词后，正式程度中等。

例句：The lesion was not a cyst as such, but a small fluid-filled area.


**not normal for you** 对你来说不正常；与个人常态不同

在健康检查语境中强调以个人基线为参照，而非与他人比较。

例句：Contact a doctor if you notice any breast change that is not normal for you.
