## 长难句理解

> The data taken includes telephone numbers and email addresses relating to individuals and organisations but does not include bank details or other sensitive information.

**语法要点：**Past participle as post-modifier; compound predicates

**分析：**过去分词短语`taken`作定语修饰`data`，并列谓语`includes...but does not include`，`relating to`作后置定语。

**翻译：**被窃取的数据包括与个人和组织相关的电话号码和电子邮件地址，但不包括银行详细信息或其他敏感信息。


> The DfE confirmed the incident and said it is working closely with the National Cyber Security Centre and the National Crime Agency (NCA).

**语法要点：**Compound predicate with object clause

**分析：**主语后接并列谓语，宾语从句`it is working...`用进行时表当前持续的行动。

**翻译：**教育部证实了该事件，并表示正与国家网络安全中心和国家犯罪局紧密合作。


> It stressed the 607,000 figure relates to the total number of records affected, not individuals.

**语法要点：**Ellipsis of 'that' in object clause; past participle post-modifier

**分析：**`figure relates to...`为宾语从句，省略了引导词`that`；`affected`为过去分词后置定语修饰`records`。

**翻译：**该部门强调，60.7万这一数字指的是受影响的记录总数，而非个人数量。


> Around a quarter of further education institutions (24%) reported experiencing a breach or attack at least weekly according to the government's most recent Cyber Security Breaches Survey.

**语法要点：**Gerund after 'report'; compound preposition 'according to'

**分析：**主语包含分数表达`a quarter of...`，谓语`reported experiencing...`是接动名词作宾语，后面有`according to`引出消息来源。

**翻译：**根据政府最新的《网络安全漏洞调查》，约四分之一的继续教育机构（24%）报告至少每周遭遇一次破坏或攻击。

## 习语与地道表达

**in place** 到位，已建立

常与`have`或`put`连用，表示措施或系统已建立并运行。

例句：Emergency procedures are in place to ensure everyone's safety.


**at least** 至少

用于表示最低限度、数量或程度。

例句：You should drink at least eight glasses of water a day.


**weak spots** 弱点，薄弱环节

可指物理、系统或个人的易受攻击之处。

例句：Regular security checks help identify weak spots in the company's IT infrastructure.


**for fun** 为了好玩

表示做某事是出于娱乐而非认真目的。

例句：Many people learn to play musical instruments just for fun.
