## Reading Passage

Hackers have obtained about 607,000 records in a cyber-attack on the Department for Education (DfE) in England.

The data taken includes telephone numbers and email addresses relating to individuals and organisations but does not include bank details or other sensitive information.

The DfE confirmed the incident and said it is working closely with the National Cyber Security Centre and the National Crime Agency (NCA).

The data protection risk to individuals is not considered high and it is understood the attack was contained quickly.

The Turing Scheme portal - a programme providing funding for international education - and the DfE online help desk have both been affected, but are expected to be operating normally later this week.

The DfE has referred itself to the Information Commissioner's Office.

"We have robust processes in place to protect information and took swift action to contain this incident", a DfE spokesperson said.

"The information involved is limited to customer service contact details relating to individuals and organisations. No other data has been accessed".

It stressed the 607,000 figure relates to the total number of records affected, not individuals.

An NCA spokesperson said: "We are aware of an incident affecting the Department for Education and are working with partners to understand the circumstances and impact."

Cyber incidents within the education sector are becoming more frequent.

Around a quarter of further education institutions (24%) reported experiencing a breach or attack at least weekly according to the government's most recent Cyber Security Breaches Survey., external

And more than half of schools have reported an attack or breach in the last year.

## 长难句理解

> The data taken includes telephone numbers and email addresses relating to individuals and organisations but does not include bank details or other sensitive information.

**语法要点：**Past and present participles as postmodifiers; coordinate contrast with but

**分析：**主句为 `The data taken includes...`，`taken` 是过去分词作后置定语修饰 `data`；`relating to...` 是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 `telephone numbers and email addresses`。`but does not include...` 与前面的谓语形成转折并列，明确哪些信息未被获取。可借鉴 `名词+过去分词/现在分词短语` 作定语的紧凑写法。

**翻译：**被获取的数据包括与个人和组织相关的电话号码和电子邮件地址，但不包括银行账户详情或其他敏感信息。


> The data protection risk to individuals is not considered high and it is understood the attack was contained quickly.

**语法要点：**Passive reporting and anticipatory it

**分析：**该句由 `and` 连接两个并列分句。第一分句使用被动结构 `is not considered high` 表达评估结果；第二分句 `it is understood` 是形式主语结构，后面省略了 `that` 的从句 `the attack was contained quickly` 是真正主语。整体用于客观转述已知情况和官方判断。

**翻译：**对个人造成的数据保护风险被认为不高；据了解，攻击已被迅速控制。


> The Turing Scheme portal - a programme providing funding for international education - and the DfE online help desk have both been affected, but are expected to be operating normally later this week.

**语法要点：**Apposition, present participle phrase, ellipsis in coordinate clause, passive

**分析：**主语是 `The Turing Scheme portal and the DfE online help desk`，中间破折号之间的 `a programme providing funding for international education` 是同位语，补充说明 `The Turing Scheme portal`；`providing...` 为现在分词短语后置修饰 `programme`。谓语 `have both been affected` 为现在完成时被动；`but are expected...` 省略了主语，形成转折，表达受影响但仍预计恢复。

**翻译：**图灵计划门户——一个为国际教育提供资助的项目——和 DfE 在线服务台均受到影响，但预计本周晚些时候将恢复正常运行。


> It stressed the 607,000 figure relates to the total number of records affected, not individuals.

**语法要点：**Omitted that-clause after reporting verb; past participle postmodifier; contrastive ellipsis

**分析：**主句为 `It stressed`，`stressed` 后省略了引导词 `that`，接宾语从句 `the 607,000 figure relates to...`。从句中 `affected` 是过去分词作后置定语，修饰 `records`；`not individuals` 为对比省略，完整为 `not the number of individuals`，强调统计口径。

**翻译：**该部门强调，607,000 这个数字涉及受影响记录的总数，而不是受影响个人的数量。

## 习语与地道表达

**weak spots** 薄弱环节；易受攻击之处

比喻性表达，常用于安全、商业或系统分析语境；通常用复数，单数形式为 `weak spot`。

例句：The audit identified weak spots in the campus network that could be exploited by attackers.


**true cost** 真实代价；全部影响

用于强调表面成本之外的更广泛后果，常见于新闻报道和商业分析，常与 `of` 连用。

例句：The report examines the true cost of repeated data breaches to public trust.


**for 'fun'** 出于好玩；闹着玩

非正式表达；原文引号表示这是被报道的动机，用于说明行为并非出于经济或恶意目的，有时带有批评意味。

例句：Some students admitted they had tried to bypass the filter for fun, not for financial gain.
