## Reading Passage

HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC) sent more than 81,000 letters warning cryptocurrency holders they may owe capital gains tax in the past year.

A Freedom of Information (FOI) request seen by the BBC showed the number of letters sent by the tax authority had almost tripled since 2024.

Investors could face fines or prosecution if they fail to declare profits they made from selling, even if they exchange one cryptocurrency for another.

New powers due to be given to HMRC next year will make it easier to target wealthy crypto investors, with one analyst saying investigations will be like "shooting fish in a barrel".

"There is the expectation amongst tax authorities that cryptocurrency investment is rife with tax evasion," says Neela Chauhan, partner at UHY Hacker Young - which carried out the FOI.

"A lot of the traders are young, have had little previous exposure to HMRC and often work under the assumption that HMRC has limited visibility over their activities."

In the 2025-26 financial year, HMRC sent 81,172 warning letters, emails and text messages to crypto investors it suspects may have underpaid tax.

An HMRC spokesperson said: "We’re committed to helping people pay the right amount of tax, and the vast majority do.

"We regularly send letters to educate, remind or prompt customers to review their tax affairs, including customers who use crypto assets."

Accountants are urging investors to check whether they owe money, as upcoming powers will make it easier for HMRC to target individuals.

From March 2027, cryptocurrency platforms located in dozens of countries outside the UK will be obliged to share information about their customers with tax authorities.

HMRC said the new powers would force "crypto bros to pay their fair share of tax" when the rules were announced last year.

The tax office estimated the changes would help raise up to £315m by April 2030, adding that this was the same amount needed to fund more than 10,000 newly qualified nurses for a year.

## 长难句理解

> A Freedom of Information (FOI) request seen by the BBC showed the number of letters sent by the tax authority had almost tripled since 2024.

**语法要点：**宾语从句；过去分词短语作后置定语；过去完成时

**分析：**主句为 `A Freedom of Information (FOI) request ... showed`，后接宾语从句 `the number ... had almost tripled`。主语后跟过去分词短语 `seen by the BBC` 作后置定语，宾语从句中 `letters sent by the tax authority` 也含过去分词短语作后置定语。

**翻译：**英国广播公司看到的一项信息自由申请显示，税务机关发出的信函数量自2024年以来几乎增加了两倍。


> Investors could face fines or prosecution if they fail to declare profits they made from selling, even if they exchange one cryptocurrency for another.

**语法要点：**条件状语从句；省略关系代词的定语从句；让步状语从句

**分析：**主句 `Investors could face fines or prosecution` 说明可能后果；条件状语从句 `if they fail to declare ...` 说明触发条件；`profits they made from selling` 中包含省略关系代词的定语从句；`even if` 引导让步状语从句，表示即便换币也算处置。

**翻译：**投资者如果未申报出售所得的利润，可能面临罚款或起诉，即使只是把一种加密货币换成另一种。


> New powers due to be given to HMRC next year will make it easier to target wealthy crypto investors, with one analyst saying investigations will be like 'shooting fish in a barrel'.

**语法要点：**形容词短语作后置定语；形式宾语 it；with + 名词 + 现在分词结构

**分析：**主句为 `New powers ... will make it easier ...`，其中 `due to be given to HMRC next year` 是形容词短语作后置定语，表示预计明年授予；`with one analyst saying ...` 为独立主格结构，补充分析师的评论，其中含引语 `shooting fish in a barrel`。

**翻译：**将于明年授予英国税务海关总署的新权力会使针对富有加密货币投资者的调查更容易，一位分析师称到时调查将如同“瓮中捉鳖”。


> A lot of the traders are young, have had little previous exposure to HMRC and often work under the assumption that HMRC has limited visibility over their activities.

**语法要点：**并列谓语；同位语从句；现在完成时

**分析：**引语中三个并列谓语 `are young`、`have had little previous exposure`、`often work under the assumption` 描述交易者特征；`that` 引导同位语从句，说明 `assumption` 的具体内容；从句中 `has limited visibility over` 表示掌握信息有限。

**翻译：**许多交易者很年轻，此前很少与英国税务海关总署打过交道，而且常常想当然地认为该机构对他们的活动了解有限。

## 习语与地道表达

**shooting fish in a barrel** 瓮中捉鳖；用来形容事情非常容易，常略带贬义

非正式口语表达，常用于描述在条件极为有利时轻易完成任务；可单独作表语，也可与 `like` 连用。

例句：With all the transaction records now available, identifying underreporting became like shooting fish in a barrel.


**pay one's fair share** 缴纳自己应承担的那一份（税款、费用等）

常用在税收或费用分担话题中，语气强调公平；`one's` 需根据主语替换为 `his/her/their` 等。

例句：The new reporting rules are intended to ensure that crypto investors pay their fair share.


**crypto bros** 加密货币兄弟；指热情炒作加密货币、风格高调且常被视为缺乏风险意识的年轻男性投资者

非正式且略带贬义，多用于新闻报道或社交媒体评论；不适合正式书面语。

例句：The regulator's message was aimed at crypto bros who think they can ignore tax rules.
