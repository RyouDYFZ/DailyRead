## 长难句理解

> It doesn't take as much imagination as it used to for [us] to imagine how this could go wrong.

**语法要点：**Dummy 'it' + comparative 'as much...as' + infinitive subject

**分析：**主句是 “It doesn't take as much imagination as it used to”，其中 “it” 为形式主语，真正的主语是后面的不定式短语 “to imagine how this could go wrong”。比较结构 “as much...as it used to” 表示现在所需的想象程度低于过去，说明风险变得更容易设想。

**翻译：**与过去相比，我们不需要那么丰富的想象力就能设想这件事会如何出错。


> It was the first time he had spoken publicly since a post went viral last week by a researcher who quit the AI firm Anthropic.

**语法要点：**It was the first time + past perfect; since-clause; reduced relative clause

**分析：**主句为 “It was the first time”，后接省略 that 的定语从句 “he had spoken publicly”；过去完成时 “had spoken” 强调在 “was the first time” 之前发生的动作。“since” 引导时间状语从句 “a post went viral”，其中过去分词短语 “by a researcher” 和定语从句 “who quit...” 修饰 “a post”。

**翻译：**这是他自上周一名辞职离开 Anthropic 的研究员的帖子引发病毒式传播以来首次公开发言。


> After Altman's comments, Meta boss Mark Zuckerberg wrote on X that every AI lab has the ability and the incentive 'to take its own actions' towards making AI tools and models that are designed for safety.

**语法要点：**Prepositional time phrase + main clause + that-clause + infinitive of purpose/attribute

**分析：**主句为 “Mark Zuckerberg wrote”。“After Altman's comments” 是时间状语；“that” 引导宾语从句，其中 “to take its own actions” 是不定式短语作定语修饰 ability 和 incentive；“towards making...” 是介词短语表方向；“that are designed for safety” 为定语从句修饰 AI tools and models。

**翻译：**在 Altman 发表上述言论后，Meta 首席执行官马克·扎克伯格在 X 上写道，每家 AI 实验室都有能力和动力“采取自己的行动”，以开发为安全而设计的 AI 工具和模型。


> Jack Clark, an Anthropic executive and co-founder, told the BBC on Monday that leaving AI to be a "totally unregulated industry" was "rolling dice with immense risks".

**语法要点：**Appositive phrase + main clause + that-clause with gerund subject

**分析：**主句为 “Jack Clark told the BBC”。“an Anthropic executive and co-founder” 是主语的同位语；“that” 引导宾语从句，其主语是动名词短语 “leaving AI to be a 'totally unregulated industry'”，谓语为 “was 'rolling dice with immense risks'”，使用引号内容作表语，表示比喻性判断。

**翻译：**Anthropic 高管兼联合创始人杰克·克拉克周一告诉 BBC，让 AI 成为一个“完全不受监管的行业”就像“拿巨大风险掷骰子”。

## 习语与地道表达

**roll the dice** 孤注一掷；冒险做结果难以预料的事

比喻性表达，常用于批评某个决定风险很高，如文中 “rolling dice with immense risks” 表示把极大风险当作赌注。

例句：The board decided to roll the dice on an unproven AI model.


**jump on** 急切地利用（某事物），往往带有投机意味

常见于商业和媒体语境中，常以被动形式 “be jumped on” 出现，表示利用某个热点或恐惧情绪来制造声势。

例句：Some critics jumped on the safety fears to attract attention to their own platforms.


**strike someone as** 给某人留下……的印象

后常接形容词或名词短语，如 “strike someone as a bad idea”，语气较正式，多用于评论观点。

例句：The proposal struck many employees as unnecessarily risky.


**way ahead of** 远远领先于；比……超前得多

口语化程度较高，常与 “be/keep/stay” 连用，表示在某方面明显领先。文中指让安全与对齐在能力之前。

例句：Their safety testing is way ahead of their competitors' current practices.
