## 文章摘要

`WhatsApp` 宣布为青少年引入可选的家长控制功能，家长可以获知孩子加入或退出群组、管理隐私设置并限制 `Meta AI` 内容，但无法查看消息和通话内容。分析师 `Matt Navarra` 认为“可选”性质是明显弱点，并质疑是否有足够家庭会实际使用。该变化发生在英国政府计划从 2027 年 3 月起禁止 16 岁以下青少年使用社交媒体平台的监管压力之下；`WhatsApp` 目前作为消息服务被排除在禁令之外。

## Reading Passage

WhatsApp is introducing new parental controls for teenagers, as tech companies continue to come under pressure to provide more age-appropriate experiences for children.

The social messaging app's new tools will give parents a range of options, such as being told when their child joins or leaves a group. It will also let them decide on privacy settings for their account.

In a blog post, WhatsApp, clarified parents would not be given the power to see the messages their teen is sending and receiving, which along with calls would stay private and protected by end-to-end encryption.

And it said the controls were optional, which social media analyst Matt Navarra called an "obvious weakness".

He said the move was "fairly convenient" for Meta, which owns WhatsApp, because it reduced the company's reliance on age verification by putting some responsibility on parents and teenagers.

"Meta is trying to show regulators we have a child safety system, we have parental controls and we're rolling it out across our platforms," he said.

"I think the big unanswered question is whether enough families will actually use it."

The minimum age to use WhatsApp is 13 in the UK, although it is higher in some countries.

According to the firm, parents will be able to adjust or switch off the controls at any time.

The settings will be "straightforward to set up" using a Pin set by a parent, which teens will need if they want to switch off the controls.

Some of the new privacy tools include selecting who can see a child's profile photo and who can add them to groups.

Parents can also choose how their teenager uses Channels, which allows organisations and public figures to send out content.

And they will have the option of choosing between the default 13+ setting for Meta AI, the app's optional built-in AI assistant, or a stricter "Limited Content" option.

"All of these controls are being designed to support open conversations about how to use WhatsApp between parents and teens, while keeping everyone's personal messages private," the company said.

The changes come as pressure mounts on tech companies from governments and regulators to change the way they operate in order to better protect children using their platforms.

In June, the government announced it plans to ban under-16s from social media platforms, with the restrictions expected to come into force in March 2027.

It said "highly effective age assurance" measures would be used to check the age of those using social media.

WhatsApp is currently excluded from the ban because the government considers it a messaging service.

## 长难句理解

> WhatsApp is introducing new parental controls for teenagers, as tech companies continue to come under pressure to provide more age-appropriate experiences for children.

**语法要点：**现在进行时 + `as` 原因状语从句 + `continue to do`

**分析：**主句为 `WhatsApp is introducing new parental controls for teenagers`，用现在进行时说明平台正在推出新功能；`as` 引导原因状语从句，指出推出背景是科技公司持续承受压力。`continue to come under pressure` 表示压力持续存在，后面的 `to provide...` 说明压力的目标。

**翻译：**WhatsApp 正在为青少年推出新的家长控制功能，因为科技公司持续面临提供更适龄体验的压力。


> He said the move was "fairly convenient" for Meta, which owns WhatsApp, because it reduced the company's reliance on age verification by putting some responsibility on parents and teenagers.

**语法要点：**宾语从句 + 非限制性定语从句 + `because` 原因状语 + `by doing` 方式状语

**分析：**主句为 `He said...`，后接省略 `that` 的宾语从句 `the move was "fairly convenient" for Meta`；`which owns WhatsApp` 是非限制性定语从句，补充说明 `Meta`；`because it reduced...` 是原因状语从句，解释为什么对 Meta 有利；`by putting...` 是介词短语，说明降低依赖的方式。

**翻译：**他表示，这一举措对拥有 `WhatsApp` 的 `Meta` 来说“相当方便”，因为它通过把部分责任交给家长和青少年，减少了对年龄验证的依赖。


> The settings will be "straightforward to set up" using a Pin set by a parent, which teens will need if they want to switch off the controls.

**语法要点：**现在分词短语作方式状语 + 过去分词后置定语 + `which` 非限制性定语从句 + `if` 条件句

**分析：**主句为 `The settings will be "straightforward to set up"`；`using a Pin set by a parent` 是现在分词短语作方式状语，其中 `set by a parent` 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 `Pin`；`which teens will need` 是非限制性定语从句，修饰 `a Pin`；`if they want to switch off the controls` 是条件状语从句。整句说明设置的简单性及关闭控制所需条件。

**翻译：**这些设置“很容易设置”，使用家长设定的 PIN，青少年若想关闭这些控制就需要该 PIN。


> The changes come as pressure mounts on tech companies from governments and regulators to change the way they operate in order to better protect children using their platforms.

**语法要点：**`as` 状语从句 + `to` 不定式目的 + `the way` 定语从句 + 现在分词后置定语

**分析：**主句为 `The changes come`；`as` 引导时间/原因状语从句，说明变化发生的背景是压力增大；`from governments and regulators` 说明压力来源；`to change the way they operate` 表示压力的要求，其中 `they operate` 是修饰 `the way` 的定语从句；`in order to better protect...` 表示目的；`using their platforms` 是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 `children`。

**翻译：**这些变化发生之际，来自政府和监管机构的压力不断上升，要求科技公司改变其运作方式，以更好地保护使用其平台的儿童。

## 习语与地道表达

**come under pressure** 承受压力；面临来自……的压力

中性新闻用语，常接 `to do something` 表示压力指向的行动，也可与 `from` 连用说明压力来源；不用作被动语态。

例句：Universities have come under pressure to provide clearer information about tuition fees.


**pressure mounts on** 压力不断增加；压力集中在……上

常用于新闻报道，说明问题或批评逐渐积聚；常以 `as` 引导时间或原因背景。

例句：Pressure is mounting on exam boards to address the rise in appeals.


**come into force** 生效；开始实施

正式用语，多用于法律、规则或限制；主语通常是 `new rules`、`restrictions`、`law` 等，不能使用被动。

例句：The new attendance policy will come into force at the start of the autumn term.


**switch off** 关掉；关闭（设备或功能）

口语与说明文都可用；在文中指关闭家长控制，也可用于关闭设备或通知。

例句：If students want to switch off notifications, they can change the app settings.
