## Reading Passage

Beavers have been described as 'ecosystem engineers' that can help boost bio-diversity and drought resilience

Supporters of a plan to reintroduce beavers to a county say they could help landscapes become more resilient to drought and climate change.

Gloucestershire Wildlife Trust and Forestry England are surveying different parts of the county to assess their suitability for a new reintroduction programme.

"Beavers are a keystone species" said Emily Bowen of the Beaver Trust. "They are ecosystem engineers, which means that they can have a massive impact on the landscape around them."

The Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Defra), which would have to approve the measure, said it could make a "huge difference to nature recovery".

"By changing the land to suit their needs, they can change it in such a way that boosts biodiversity and increases resilience in terms of climate change," Bowen added.

As semi-aquatic mammals, beavers are known for building dams that create ponds and wetlands.

Even small streams can feed a substantial wetland habitat that will then support an abundant array of biodiversity.

Beavers are a native species, but have not been a regular sight in the west of England for around 400 years, after hunting for food and medical ingredients forced their numbers to dramatically decline.

A Defra spokesperson said: "Reintroducing beavers has the potential to make a huge difference to nature recovery, and we are proud to champion this work.

"We will continue to support our partners and ensure that the return of beavers benefits both wildlife and communities."

Beavers were introduced into an enclosure in the Forest of Dean in 2018

Beavers have already been living at Greathough Brook in the Forest of Dean since 2018 and regular surveys have revealed a dramatic increase in biodiversity.

"I've seen stoats, I've seen weasels, I've even seen pine martens," said Kate Wollen, from Forestry England.

"The beavers have created this amazing habitat that has brought in all the insects that are at the bottom of the food chain and then it brings in all the other animals that then feed on them, it's just a whole wonderful network of habitats."

Kate Wollen from Forestry England describes the transformation as a "watery wonderland"

A new re-introduction programme in the Severn Vale area would require a government licence.

Studies are being carried out to find suitable locations and a consultation is underway with landowners and users who might be affected by the change in the landscape.

"We have to make sure that we are working really closely with people who live, work and manage the areas that we're thinking about putting beavers," said Mega Adamou, of Gloucestershire Wildlife Trust.

"We'll be seeing more complex wetland habitats being created," she added.

"We'll be seeing a little bit more resilience to drought in those areas where the beavers might end up living."

"It's been really interesting to see how the general public have taken to beavers and getting people thinking about seeing them back in our countryside."

There have been three licensed releases of beavers in England to date, with about 100 animals expected to be released this year.

## 长难句理解

> As semi-aquatic mammals, beavers are known for building dams that create ponds and wetlands.

**语法要点：**状语 + 主句 + 定语从句

**分析：**主句为 `beavers are known for building dams`。句首 `As semi-aquatic mammals` 作状语，说明海狸的类别；`that create ponds and wetlands` 为定语从句，修饰 `dams`，说明堤坝的作用。整体逻辑：先给出类别特征，再说明其习性和结果。

**翻译：**作为半水生哺乳动物，海狸以建造水坝而闻名，这些水坝会形成池塘和湿地。


> "By changing the land to suit their needs, they can change it in such a way that boosts biodiversity and increases resilience in terms of climate change," Bowen added.

**语法要点：**方式状语 + 主句 + 结果状语从句（such...that）

**分析：**主句为 `they can change it in such a way`。句首 `By changing...` 作方式状语；`that boosts biodiversity and increases resilience...` 为结果状语从句，说明如此改变土地所产生的后果。`in such a way that` 是固定结构，表示“以这样的方式以至于”。

**翻译：**鲍恩补充说，通过改变土地来满足自身需求，它们能够以促进生物多样性并增强气候变化适应力的方式改变土地。


> Even small streams can feed a substantial wetland habitat that will then support an abundant array of biodiversity.

**语法要点：**强调副词 + 主句 + 定语从句

**分析：**主句为 `Even small streams can feed a substantial wetland habitat`；`that will then support an abundant array of biodiversity` 为定语从句，修饰 `habitat`，说明湿地在形成后能够支撑丰富的生物多样性。`Even` 置于句首起强调作用，表示“即使是”。

**翻译：**即使是小溪也能滋养出大片湿地栖息地，而这些湿地随后将支撑起丰富的生物多样性。


> Beavers are a native species, but have not been a regular sight in the west of England for around 400 years, after hunting for food and medical ingredients forced their numbers to dramatically decline.

**语法要点：**并列句 + 时间状语从句

**分析：**主句为 `Beavers are a native species, but have not been a regular sight...`，由 `but` 连接两个并列谓语；`after hunting... forced their numbers to dramatically decline` 为时间状语从句，说明数量锐减的原因。`forced` 后接宾语 + 不定式作宾补，`dramatically decline` 为不定式短语。

**翻译：**海狸是本地物种，但约400年来在英格兰西部已不常见，原因是人们为获取食物和药材而猎杀它们，导致其数量急剧下降。
